06 / Reflection

Knowing what to hand over

I started by looking for ways to make production faster. Across 45 timers, I became more interested in deciding where a tool helped and where I needed to step back in.

01

What worked

Reusable setup gave me a common starting point. Original artwork, selected motion and manual composition let each timer keep its own character. Short clips became more useful when I could sequence them myself.

02

What stayed difficult

A convincing generation could still change the background, add an object or move too much. I had to judge each take in the scene. Some ideas needed several attempts; others were quicker to make myself.

03

What I’d change next time

I’d test the hardest movement earlier, before developing the rest of the scene. I’d also keep a clearer record of rejected takes and why they failed, so those lessons could carry into future experiments.

✳

What I’d carry into the next project is that judgment: when to draw, generate, build a small tool, or finish something by hand.
