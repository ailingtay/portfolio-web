It worked.
I missed the craft.

Using Claude, I built a browser tool that generated and exported a 16:9 timer. Setup was quick, but typography, animation and the finish felt too constrained.

04 Review, reject, refine

Knowing what
to keep.

A generated clip can look impressive and still be wrong for the job. For a classroom timer, I wanted movement that felt alive without becoming distracting.

RunwayML / Connected motion experiments

An image is a starting point.
Direction makes it move.

Connect a reference to a generation, describe the movement, then compare the results. The canvas keeps the experiments together; the final choice stays mine.

05 Think beyond the short clip

A few seconds.
A longer story.

Short generations gave me useful movement. The timers needed minutes. Generating the whole duration would mean more cost, more waiting and less control over pacing.
